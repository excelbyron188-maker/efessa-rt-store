GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How do I choose the right size?

Check the product's size information and contact support if you need help. Add accurate garment measurements before launch.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery windows depend on location and fulfilment arrangements. Publish verified delivery estimates and fees before accepting real orders.

Can I exchange an item?

Exchange eligibility and timing must follow the store's published returns policy. Confirm the actual rules with EFESSA RT before launch.

Can I track my order?

This prototype can display demonstration orders saved in the same browser. Real order tracking requires a database and backend.

Can I pay online?

No real payment is processed by this prototype. A payment provider and secure server-side verification must be integrated before launch.
